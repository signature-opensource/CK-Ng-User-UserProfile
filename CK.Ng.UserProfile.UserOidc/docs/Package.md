Adds the OpenID Connect section ("Linked accounts") to the "Sign-in methods" tab of the CK.Ng.UserProfile
`/profile` page: the user lists its identities and what the providers shared, unlinks them, and links
another provider (a remote login that binds the identity to the user). Unlinking and linking require a
recent login.

See the [README](https://github.com/signature-opensource/CK-Ng-User-UserProfile/blob/stable/CK.Ng.UserProfile.UserOidc/README.md).
